Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about Islamic financing

What is Halal or Islamic financing?

Halal financing follows Sharia law which prohibits interest (Riba). Instead, we use models like Murabaha, Mudarabah, and Ijara where profit is earned through trade, partnership, or leasing — not interest.

Can non-Muslims apply for loans?

Yes, absolutely! All religions are welcome to apply. Our financing is ethical and transparent for everyone.

What is the maximum loan amount?

We offer financing from ₹10,000 up to ₹1 Crore depending on the loan type and eligibility criteria.

What documents are required?

Basic KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN), income proof, and purpose of loan. Our advisor will guide you through the exact requirements.

Is there any processing fee?

Yes, there is a nominal processing fee of ₹1,750 for all loan types. There are no hidden charges whatsoever.

How long does loan approval take?

Once all documents are submitted, approval typically takes 3–5 working days. Disbursement happens within 7 working days after approval.
